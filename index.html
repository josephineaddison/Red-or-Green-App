Red-Green Color Analysis

This app takes a picture with your device's camera and then displays whether a color is more similar to red or green when the user clicks the image.

Press the "choose file" button to create or choose image file:



Touch the picture to see an output of your RGB, HSV, and red-green values.

This area will be replaced by the RGB values once the image is tapped.

This area will be replaced by the HSV values once the image is tapped.

This area will be replaced by red or green once the image is tapped.
